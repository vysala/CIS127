New Thai Elephant Restaurant Review

Rating: ★ ★ ★ ★ ★

Update rating:

Favorite dish: Mixed sashimi

Review: Unbeatable prices for delicious Thai food. Best part is that their curries come with rice and it's not an extra fee. I think that their Roast Duck Curry is by far the best dish, but they have an extensive menu.The service was some of the best service we've ever had. The restaurant's staff was amazing. We really loved the green curry and the pad kee mao. The service was also pretty good and the curry puff was a surprise addition in a Thai place.
