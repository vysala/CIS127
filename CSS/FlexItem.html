How the Flex Properties Work

flex-basis sets the initial size of each flex item.

  • Item 1 starts at 200px.
  • Item 2 starts at 100px.
  • Item 3 starts at 100px.

flex-grow determines how extra space is distributed.

  • Item 1 has flex-grow: 1.
  • Item 2 has flex-grow: 2.
  • Item 3 has flex-grow: 1.

Therefore, Item 2 receives twice as much of the extra space as Item 1 and Item 3.

flex-shrink determines how items behave when the container becomes too small.

Since all three items have flex-shrink: 1, they are allowed to shrink when necessary.

Remember

flex-basis = starting size

flex-grow = how much extra space to take

flex-shrink = how much to shrink
